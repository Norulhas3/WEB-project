Frequently Asked Questions

Who can enter?

Any squad of 4 enrolled university students (plus up to 2 substitutes). Student ID is verified by the captain at check-in.

Is there an entry fee?

No. Open qualifier entry is free for all registered squads.

What happens if I get disqualified?

Third-party overlays, macros or modified files lead to instant disqualification. See the rulebook on the Media & Intel page.

FPP or TPP?

You choose your preferred mode during registration. FPP is the competitive standard.
