Rule 1.1 (Student Status): All active roster members must be officially enrolled students with valid credentials.

Rule 1.2 (Check-in Protocol): Team captains must check into the tournament lobby channel 30 minutes before match start.

Rule 1.3 (Hardware Integrity): Matches are played on supervised university systems or verified personal devices.

Rule 1.4 (Zero Tolerance): Third-party overlays, macro scripts, or modified game files lead to instant disqualification.

Rule 1.5 (Scoring Calculation): Placement points are awarded per round alongside 1 point per confirmed squad elimination.
